The problem

How can portfolio managers see where default risk, outstanding exposure and realized loss concentrate—and turn that evidence into controlled credit decisions?

Millions of loans differ by borrower profile, purpose, grade, term, pricing and outcome. A consistent analytical layer is needed to align portfolio KPIs, risk segmentation, PD monitoring and executive reporting.

Dataset

2.26MClean loan records
151Source features
$34.0BFunded amount

LendingClub accepted loans from 2007–2018 Q4. Supervised modeling uses 1.35M loans with defensible terminal outcomes; unresolved statuses are excluded to reduce target censoring.

Approach

02

03

Decision reporting

Connect exposure, delinquency, loss, model risk and risk-appetite scenarios in six dashboard pages.

Model / analysis

Logistic Regression PD model

A leakage-audited, interpretable probability-of-default pipeline uses median imputation, standardization, categorical encoding and balanced class weights. It supports ranking, segmentation and watchlist prioritization—not automated adverse decisions.

Recall0.706

Key insights

Portfolio health is amber / elevated.

Default and loss are material while $9.51B of principal remains outstanding.

Risk concentration is economically meaningful.

36.77% of loans sit in High or Very High model-risk bands; PD must be read together with exposure and delinquency.

Pricing cannot repair weak affordability.

Higher rates alone do not compensate for adverse FICO, DTI, term and purpose combinations.

Recommendations

  • Prioritize reviews using predicted PD + outstanding principal + delinquency + segment risk.
  • Test stronger verification and tighter limits for weak-FICO/high-DTI and high-risk 60-month segments.
  • Use the Balanced risk-appetite scenario as a backtest baseline, not an automatic production policy.
  • Price for expected loss, funding, operating cost, capital charge and target margin.

Limitations

  • Accepted-loan data excludes rejected applicants and creates selection bias.
  • 2007–2018 outcomes may not represent current products, borrowers or economic conditions.
  • No monthly status snapshots means roll rates, cures and migrations cannot be estimated.
  • Moderate discrimination and substantial false positives require human review and governance.
