SaaS Product Analytics
& Churn.

Power BI · SQL · Python · Machine Learning

The problem

How can a B2B SaaS team identify churn risk early, understand its drivers and turn customer signals into prioritized retention actions?

Revenue, subscription and product-usage data lived in separate views. Growth could mask weakening adoption, while account teams lacked one reliable way to connect customer health, predicted churn and recurring revenue at risk.

The model workflow produces account-level churn probabilities and interpretable risk drivers. Evaluation artifacts and model-performance metrics are maintained with the repository outputs.

Key insights

Growth can hide churn pressure.

MRR and active-account trends need to be read alongside forward churn and expected revenue at risk.

Product health creates early warning signals.

Low adoption, billing risk and high friction help identify accounts requiring proactive intervention.

Retention works best when it is prioritized.

Combining churn probability, explainability and MRR exposure focuses customer-success effort where it matters most.
