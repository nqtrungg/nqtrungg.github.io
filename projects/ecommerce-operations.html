The problem

Which products, SKUs and channels create profitable, sustainable growth after returns, inventory risk and channel costs?

Revenue alone does not reveal operational quality. Teams need a shared view of margin leakage, returns, stockout exposure, overstock and channel economics to prioritize action at product and SKU level.

  • Which products and categories drive revenue and margin?
  • Where are returns and refunds reducing revenue quality?
  • Which SKUs need replenishment, rebalancing or purchasing reduction?
  • Which channels remain attractive after commissions and returns?

Dataset

Public Olist transaction data forms the commercial foundation. A transparent generated Operations layer adds inventory snapshots, channels, warehouses, returns, refunds, lead times, reorder points, stockout risk and overstock flags.

Key insights

Revenue is concentrated.

Home & Furniture is the largest category group and Marketplace is the largest revenue channel.

Margin quality needs context.

Gross margin is healthy, but commissions and refunds materially reduce net revenue quality.

Returns are material.

10,775 returned units and $1.28M in refunds require product, seller and reason-level investigation.

Inventory needs two-sided action.

Critical stockout exposure requires replenishment while overstock calls for markdown, transfer or purchasing reduction.

Recommendations

  • Review high-revenue/low-margin products for pricing, cost, quality and assortment changes.
  • Prioritize critical and high-risk SKUs for replenishment using lead time and days of inventory.
  • Investigate repeated quality, damaged-item and wrong-item returns by product and seller.
  • Compare channel growth using net revenue after commission and returns - not gross revenue alone.

Limitations

  • Inventory, return and channel attributes are simulated for portfolio demonstration.
  • The dashboard does not represent a live ERP/WMS or real operational deployment.
  • Rule thresholds require calibration to service-level, lead-time and working-capital targets.
  • Measured business impact is not available; recommendations are analytical decision support.
