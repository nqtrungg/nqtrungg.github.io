The problem

How can an e-commerce team connect revenue, profitability, product, inventory, returns and channel performance in one decision system?

Commercial data was fragmented across operational tables. Revenue growth alone could not explain margin quality, return pressure or the product and channel drivers behind performance.

SQL validation and reusable DAX measures keep executive KPIs consistent across product, inventory, return and channel views.

Key insights

Revenue growth does not automatically mean profit growth.

Gross profit and gross margin provide the necessary context for evaluating commercial quality.

Returns are a material operational pressure point.

Return behavior should be investigated by category and product rather than treated as a single top-level rate.

Channel mix changes both scale and economics.

Channel-level analysis helps distinguish high-volume activity from profitable, sustainable performance.
