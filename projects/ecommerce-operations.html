The problem

How can an e-commerce team connect sales, margin, inventory, returns and channel cost in one operational decision system?

Separate reports make it difficult to identify profitable products, stockout exposure, overstock, return leakage and channel-quality trade-offs. This dashboard centralizes those questions into one validated model.

Key insights

Revenue is concentrated.

Home & Furniture leads category revenue, while Marketplace is the largest channel.

Healthy gross margin still faces leakage.

Commission cost and a 9.61% return rate reduce the quality of top-line growth.

Inventory requires two-sided action.

Critical stockout SKUs need replenishment while overstocked, low-velocity items tie up working capital.

Recommendations

  • Replenish critical/high-risk SKUs first; review overstock for transfer, markdown or purchasing reduction.
  • Investigate high-revenue products with weak margin or high returns for pricing, cost and quality issues.
  • Track channel performance after commission and refunds—not revenue alone.
  • Use repeated product and seller return incidents to focus root-cause reviews.

Limitations

  • Inventory, supplier, channel and return layers are synthetic business simulations.
  • Generated operational behavior should not be interpreted as Olist's real inventory performance.
  • The analysis is historical and descriptive; it does not estimate causal impact or demand forecasts.
  • Cost and profit measures depend on modeling assumptions not available in the public source.
